---
title: Gmail permissions
description: Know which Gmail capabilities map to InboxCtrl workflows.
---

InboxCtrl separates Gmail access by workflow.

## Read-only audit

Used for mailbox metadata, labels, sender review, and dry-run style workflows.

## Organizer

Used when you apply labels, archive messages, trash messages, or otherwise modify mailbox state.

## Settings and filters

Used when creating or managing Gmail filters.

The product should keep user-visible permission upgrades explicit. Demo mode simulates capabilities locally and does not request Gmail permissions.
