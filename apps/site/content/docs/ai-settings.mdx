title: AI settings
description: Configure AI features without hiding privacy choices.
---

AI features are optional. Users can keep AI disabled, use an environment provider key, or bring their own provider key where supported by the app.

## Privacy gates

Full-body AI features require explicit settings for:

- external AI processing
- full email body fetch
- AI output storage

Metadata-only workflows should stay separate from full-body workflows.

Demo mode returns deterministic AI fixture output and does not call an external AI provider.
