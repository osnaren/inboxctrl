---
title: Privacy and security
description: The launch privacy posture for self-hosted InboxCtrl.
---

InboxCtrl is self-hosted. Your deployment controls the database, tokens, provider keys, logs, backups, and network access.

## Launch posture

- Demo mode is local-only and blocks real Gmail access.
- Real mailbox mode requires Google OAuth credentials.
- Full-body AI access is gated separately from metadata access.
- BYOK secrets are encrypted before storage in the product app.
- Review-first workflows should keep rollback context where actions are reversible.

Do not expose a local development instance publicly without reviewing auth, secrets, database, and network configuration.
