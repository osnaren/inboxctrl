title: Troubleshooting
description: Common local setup and launch-foundation issues.
---

## Google redirects to the wrong URL

Check `BETTER_AUTH_URL`, the browser URL you used to open the app, and the authorized redirect URI in Google Cloud.

## The app says no Google account is connected

Confirm you are not in demo mode, then reconnect Google from the product app. Demo mode intentionally uses a local account instead of OAuth.

## AI features are disabled

Enable AI settings and privacy gates explicitly. Full-body AI routes require both external AI processing and full-body fetch to be enabled.

## Public site links to the product app

That should not happen for launch CTAs. Public pages should point to docs, source, and self-hosting guidance until a hosted product is intentionally launched.
